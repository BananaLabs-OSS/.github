# Code of Conduct

BananaLabs OSS is committed to a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free
community.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and specific.
- Respect differing backgrounds, experiences, and viewpoints.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code rather than people.
- Accept responsibility, correct mistakes, and de-escalate conflict.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, and other
abusive behavior are not acceptable in project spaces or when representing the
project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to a BananaLabs OSS organization owner
through GitHub. Maintainers may edit or remove content and may warn,
temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants when necessary. Reports
will be handled as privately and promptly as practical.
